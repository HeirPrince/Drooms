Our Story

Every Flavor Tells a Story

Nestled in the heart of Bugesera, Drooms Resort is a sanctuary of comfort, taste, and tranquility. What began as a simple passion for good food and warm hospitality has grown into a destination where culture, cuisine, and nature come together. Every meal we serve, every melody that fills the air, and every smile you share becomes part of a story that celebrates togetherness and timeless moments.

Whether you're here for a family escape, a romantic evening, or simply to unwind, our team ensures every detail reflects the spirit of Rwandan warmth and global elegance. At Drooms Resort, it’s not just about visiting — it’s about belonging.

Why Choose Us

Our Strength

  • Hygienic Food

    Your well-being is our priority. We maintain the highest standards of cleanliness in our kitchen, with all ingredients carefully handled and prepared. Every dish is crafted with a commitment to quality and safety, so you can savor your meal with complete peace of mind.

  • Fresh Environment

    Breathe in the energy! Our space is designed to be the heart of the community. With weekly live music performances, a dedicated kids' playground for endless fun, and a stylish, airy dining area, we offer a dynamic environment where every visit feels special.

  • Skilled Chefs

    Our chefs are artists of flavor. They bring years of expertise and a genuine passion for cooking to create a menu that delights the senses. From sizzling bar classics to sophisticated local dishes, every meal is a testament to their skill and dedication to delicious, high-quality food.
